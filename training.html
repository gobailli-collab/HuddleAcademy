Training and guides

Choose the starting resource for your role, then practice with your restaurant's trainer. These guides support learning conversations and should be used with your restaurant's approved procedures.

Recommended resources by role
User RoleRecommended ResourceExpected Outcome
Team memberGetting Started and first-task practiceKnow where to begin, what is expected, and who can help.
TrainerDemonstration and feedback guideDemonstrate a task and coach a supported practice attempt.
ManagerTeam learning reviewClarify ownership and identify learning gaps for follow-up.

Team members: practice with a purpose

Ask your trainer to explain the task and show a successful example. Repeat the steps in your own words, then try the task with support. Before finishing, compare your work with the expected result.

Trainers: model, assist, watch, leave

Model the task while explaining key decisions. Assist the learner during practice. Watch a complete attempt and give feedback tied to an observable action. Step back when the learner can perform the task consistently, while remaining available for questions.

Managers: make the next step clear

Assign a trainer and a specific learning objective. Confirm the resource matches your current procedure. Review the learner's demonstration and decide whether more practice or a new task is appropriate.

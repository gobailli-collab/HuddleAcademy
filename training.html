Training and guides

Choose the learning resource that matches your role. Each role uses the same basic rhythm, but the expected outcome changes based on the level of responsibility.

Role-based learning guide

Recommended Huddle Academy resources by role
User Role Recommended Resource Expected Outcome
Team Member First-task practice Complete an assigned task accurately and explain the key steps.
Trainer Demonstration and feedback guide Model the task, observe practice, and give specific coaching.
Manager Team learning review Clarify expectations, assign resources, and follow up on development.

Team member guide

Start with one task that matters on your next shift. Read the expectation, watch or observe a correct example, complete the task with a trainer nearby, and ask for one piece of feedback before repeating it independently.

Trainer guide

Explain why the task matters, demonstrate the standard, let the learner practice, and give feedback that is specific enough to use on the next attempt. The goal is confident performance, not simply finishing a training item.

Manager guide

Use Huddle resources to make expectations visible and repeatable. Assign training intentionally, confirm that leaders know what success looks like, and follow up on whether the learning is showing up during real shifts.

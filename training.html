Training and guides

Use these role-based guides to learn the essentials, model the work well, and make sure your restaurant team has consistent expectations.

Team members

As a team member, the goal is to understand your role, complete your assigned task correctly, and ask for help before mistakes become bigger problems.

Before the shift

  1. Review the task instructions and expected outcome.
  2. Confirm where the task should be completed in Huddle.
  3. Know who to ask if the process is unclear.

During practice

  1. Follow each step in order without skipping sections.
  2. Check the result against the expected standard.
  3. Ask for one correction or improvement from a trainer or supervisor.
Success looks like: finishing the task with confidence, knowing where to find support, and recognizing when a task is complete.

Trainers

Trainers help teammates learn the process by modeling the task clearly, giving practical feedback, and reinforcing the behaviors that lead to consistency.

How to teach the task

  1. Show the task once from start to finish.
  2. Explain why each step matters for the guest, manager, or team.
  3. Let the team member practice while you coach in real time.
  4. Provide one clear corrective action and one positive reinforcement point.
Best practice: model the task calmly, focus on the standard, and help the learner connect the process to the final outcome.

Managers

Managers keep onboarding structured, set expectations, and help teams maintain consistency across shifts and locations.

What to do

  1. Assign role-appropriate training tasks and define success clearly.
  2. Confirm the right access points and the correct process for each role.
  3. Schedule follow-up check-ins after the first few task completions.
  4. Document recurring questions so the support process improves over time.
Manager goal: build a repeatable training pattern that reduces confusion and helps every new hire become productive faster.

Recommended learning path

Suggested training sequence
Stage Goal Outcome
1. Prepare Confirm access and task Person understands what to do and where it happens
2. Learn Review the workflow Person knows the standard and the reason behind it
3. Practice Complete the task with coaching Person builds confidence before independent work
4. Follow up Review gaps and reinforce success Team improves with clearer expectations and fewer mistakes

Practice checklist

Use this checklist to turn a training resource into real practice. The items are intentionally simple so a team member, trainer, or manager can use them during a short coaching conversation.

Before practice

  • Confirm the exact task or skill being practiced.
  • Explain why the task matters to the shift or guest experience.
  • Review the standard or expected result.

During practice

  • Watch a correct demonstration or review the learning resource.
  • Complete the task while the trainer observes.
  • Ask one specific question about any step that was unclear.

After practice

  • Name one thing that went well.
  • Choose one improvement for the next attempt.
  • Repeat the task until the learner can complete it consistently.

Getting started with your team

Use this preparation guide before your first Huddle training session. Access and assigned tasks can differ by restaurant.

Your first three steps

  1. Confirm access. Ask your manager for the correct sign-in location and any invitation instructions.
  2. Identify your role. Know whether you are joining as a team member, trainer, or manager.
  3. Pick one task. Choose the first task you need to complete and practice it with a trainer or supervisor.

Bring one question

Write down the step you were trying to complete and what you expected to happen. A specific question helps your trainer give a useful answer.

Quick checklist:
  • I know where to sign in.
  • I know my role and task.
  • I have one question ready to ask.

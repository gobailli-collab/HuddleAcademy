Getting started with Huddle

Use this page before your first Huddle training session. Access methods and assigned tasks can differ by restaurant, so confirm local expectations with your manager or trainer.

Your first three steps

  1. Confirm access. Ask your manager for the correct sign-in location and invitation instructions.
  2. Identify your role. Know whether you are joining as a team member, trainer, or manager.
  3. Pick one task. Choose one responsibility to learn and practice before trying to master the whole platform.

What to bring

Bring your login information, the role you are training for, and one specific question. Focused questions help trainers give clearer coaching.

A simple learning rhythm

This short video reinforces the three-step process used throughout Huddle Academy: learn the task, practice it with coaching, then review what happened and ask questions.

Next step

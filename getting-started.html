Getting started with your team

Use this preparation guide before your first Huddle training session. Access and assigned tasks can differ by restaurant.

Your first three steps

  1. Confirm access. Ask your manager for the correct sign-in location and any invitation instructions. Never share your password.
  2. Confirm your role. Ask which responsibilities and learning resources apply to you.
  3. Practice one task. Have your trainer demonstrate the task, complete it with guidance, and explain what successful completion looks like.

If access is missing or your assignment is unclear, pause and ask your manager before making changes.

Bring one question

Write down the step you were trying to complete and what you expected to happen. A specific question helps your trainer give a useful answer.

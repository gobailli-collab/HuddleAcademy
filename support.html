FAQs and support

Start with these common questions. For restaurant-specific access and procedures, contact your manager or designated trainer.

Common questions

Where should I begin?

Read Getting Started, confirm your role with your manager, and choose one task to practice with your trainer.

What if I get stuck?

Bring the exact step or action you were trying to complete. A specific question leads to a more helpful answer.

How should I prepare for my first shift?

Know where to sign in, know your assigned task, and have at least one question ready to review with your trainer or manager.

What if I am not sure which role applies to me?

Ask your manager which role you are expected to complete in Huddle. Team member, trainer, and manager responsibilities are similar but not identical.

Request support

This form opens your email app so you can send a request to the support team. For actual help, contact your manager. Do not enter sensitive information.

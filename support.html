FAQs and support

Start with these common questions. For restaurant-specific access and procedures, contact your manager or designated trainer.

Common questions

Where should I begin?

Read Getting Started, confirm your role with your manager, and choose one task to practice with your trainer.

What if I cannot access Huddle?

Confirm you are using the location and account information provided by your manager. Ask your manager to check your access. Do not include passwords in a support request.

Does this academy track my progress?

This student prototype provides learning resources only. It does not create accounts or track completion.

Support request

Course demonstration only: this form does not send or store requests. For actual help, contact your manager. Do not enter sensitive information.
